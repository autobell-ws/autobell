## Executive Summary
AutoBell is the next-generation, cloud-managed SaaS IoT platform built to replace outdated, manual, and unreliable timer boxes in educational institutions. Controlled seamlessly via a Web Dashboard or Mobile App, AutoBell gives school leaders complete control over daily bell schedules, pre-announcement chimes, automated Text-to-Speech notices, and instant emergency alerts.

---

## 🚀 Core Features & Product Highlights

### 1. Cloud Remote Scheduling
*   **Access Anywhere:** Create, update, and switch bell profiles (*Normal Day*, *Exam Schedule*, *Ramadan*, *Half-Day*) from any web browser or smartphone.
*   **Instant Sync & Offline Reliability:** Schedule updates sync instantly to on-site ESP32 hardware and are cached locally in SPIFFS/LittleFS so bells ring even during internet outages.

### 2. Pre-Announcement Audio Jingle & Custom Delay
*   **Signature Campus Jingle:** Choose from a curated library of pre-announcement chimes.
*   **Configurable Pause (2–5 Seconds):** Automatically plays a pre-announcement sound followed by a customizable 2-to-5-second delay before playing any bell, speech notice, or audio broadcast.

### 3. Emergency Lockdown & Manual Triggers
*   **Instant Action:** Ring ad-hoc bells or announcements with one tap.
*   **Emergency Mode:** Authorized staff can slide to activate continuous lockdown alarms across campus, overriding regular schedules instantly.

### 4. Text-To-Speech (TTS) & Live Voice Broadcasts
*   **Automated TTS:** Type any notice into the dashboard to generate natural, crystal-clear spoken announcements.
*   **Live Broadcasts:** Send real-time voice messages or pre-recorded MP3 announcements directly to classroom speakers.

### 5. Hardware Watchdog & Zero-Downtime Reliability
*   **100% Uptime:** Built-in hardware watchdog automatically recovers the system from freezes without human intervention.
*   **Backup Device SLA:** Optional yearly agreement providing instant spare hardware replacement from central inventory.

### 6. Full System Backup & Restore
*   **Disaster Recovery:** 1-click full backup of all school records, schedules, diagnostic logs, and uploaded MP3 media files.

### 7. Partner & Reseller Program
*   **B2B Growth Engine:** Dedicated portal for school IT vendors and distributors to manage prospective leads, track deals, and earn recurring commissions.
